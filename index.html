How it works

1

Queue your thumbnails

Add a stack of candidate thumbnails to any video.

2

It runs the test

ThumbLoop starts YouTube's A/B test — your current thumbnail is never touched.

3

Winner kept automatically

It checks every 6 hours and promotes a thumbnail that leads by more than 5%. No clear winner in 24 hours? It picks the leader so things keep moving.

4

The loop continues

The next challengers from your queue start a fresh test — automatically, until your queue runs out.

FAQ

Do you get access to my YouTube account?

No. ThumbLoop runs as a Chrome extension in your own browser using your existing Studio session — we never receive your YouTube login or channel access. Your videos, queues and settings stay in your browser. The only data that ever leaves your machine is your Pro license key (sent to Lemon Squeezy to verify your subscription) and, only if you set it up yourself, swap alerts sent to your own Telegram. See our Privacy Policy.

Which videos can it test?

Public videos longer than 6 minutes that have at least one queued thumbnail. Shorts, members-only, scheduled, and private videos are skipped automatically.

Will it spam changes?

No — it checks every 6 hours and makes at most 8 changes a day, well within YouTube's limits.

How do I upgrade?

Buy a Pro license and paste the key into the extension's Settings — Pro unlocks instantly, no reinstall. Cancel and it returns to Free automatically.
